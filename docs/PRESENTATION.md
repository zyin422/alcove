## Judging checklist

- Collaboration: name who owned Launchpad vs Ledger
- Novelty: closed loop + artifact proof, not another reminder app
- Technical: working ingest, launch plan, ledger publish
- Practicality: one-click start, one-click close
- Engagement: sidecar + public feed are pleasant to use daily
- AI usage: Cursor/GrokBot for scaffolding and launch-plan/pre-draft; say it out loud (Off the Clock requirement)

## What not to say

Do not demo hard-killing apps. Do not shame language ("you failed as a person"). Fail is a status on an artifact, not a character judgment.
